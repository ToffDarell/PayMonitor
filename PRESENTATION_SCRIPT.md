# PayMonitor Presentation Script

## Presenter Script

Good day everyone. Today, we are presenting **PayMonitor**, a **multi-tenant lending cooperative management system** designed for cooperatives that need a faster, more organized, and more secure way to manage their daily lending operations.

Our system was built to solve common problems in cooperative lending such as manual borrower tracking, delayed payment monitoring, difficulty in generating reports, and limited visibility over overdue accounts. Instead of relying on scattered spreadsheets and paper records, PayMonitor brings these processes into one centralized platform.

What makes PayMonitor different is that it is not only a lending system, but also a **Software as a Service platform**. This means each cooperative gets its own isolated workspace, while the central admin can still manage subscriptions, applications, support requests, billing, and system updates from one central dashboard.

For the public side of the system, new cooperatives can visit the landing page, review available plans, and submit an application online. During application, they can choose a subscription plan and proceed with payment through **PayMongo**, which supports digital payment options. Once payment is verified and the application is approved, the cooperative can receive access to its own PayMonitor workspace.

Inside the tenant or cooperative workspace, the system focuses on the daily lending process. Users can manage **members**, create and monitor **loans**, define **loan types**, and record **loan payments**. The system automatically computes loan values such as total interest, total payable amount, monthly payment, and outstanding balance. It can also generate amortization schedules, helping staff see the full payment timeline for each borrower.

The dashboard gives the cooperative an instant summary of operations. It shows the number of active loans, total outstanding balances, overdue loans, total members, monthly collections, and recent loan activity. This allows officers and managers to quickly understand the current performance of their cooperative.

Another important feature is **overdue loan management**. When a loan becomes overdue, the system can help the cooperative respond properly by sending reminders, applying penalties, marking loans as delinquent, restructuring loans, generating demand letters, or writing off accounts when necessary. This improves collection monitoring and helps standardize how overdue cases are handled.

For organizations with multiple locations, PayMonitor also supports **branch management**. This allows cooperatives to organize operations across branches while keeping records structured and easy to monitor.

In terms of security and administration, the system supports **multi-user access** with **role-based permissions**. This means different users can be given different levels of access depending on their responsibilities. For example, an administrator, branch manager, and loan officer do not need to see or control the same parts of the system. This reduces risk and improves accountability.

The system also includes **document support** for member and loan records, **audit logs** for activity tracking, and **settings management** so each cooperative can customize details such as branding, contact information, display preferences, and security options like password updates.

For reporting, PayMonitor can generate lending reports and export them in **CSV, Excel, and PDF** formats. These reports include loan summaries, collections, overdue loans, top borrowers, and other lending insights. This helps cooperatives make better decisions and also makes reporting easier for management and compliance purposes.

On the central admin side, the platform provides tools for managing the entire SaaS environment. The super admin can review cooperative applications, approve or reject them, monitor tenants, manage plans, track billing invoices, verify payments, and oversee support concerns. There is also a **support management system** where tenants can submit concerns and the central admin can respond directly through the dashboard.

Another advanced feature of PayMonitor is its **update management system**. The platform can track releases, notify tenants of available updates, and support backup and self-update workflows. This helps keep the system maintainable and scalable as new versions are released.

Overall, PayMonitor is designed to give cooperatives a complete digital platform for lending operations while also giving the provider a reliable way to manage multiple cooperative clients in one system.

In summary, PayMonitor offers:

- Online cooperative application and subscription flow
- Multi-tenant isolated workspaces
- Member, loan, payment, and branch management
- Automated loan computation and amortization
- Overdue loan handling tools
- Role-based access and audit logging
- Billing, support, and update management
- Exportable reports for better monitoring and decision-making

With this system, cooperative operations become more efficient, transparent, and easier to scale.

Thank you, and we are now ready to demonstrate how PayMonitor works.

## Suggested Live Demo Flow

Use this order during the demo so the presentation feels smooth and realistic.

1. Show the landing page.
2. Explain that a cooperative can apply online and choose a plan.
3. Mention PayMongo payment integration for application or billing payments.
4. Switch to the central admin dashboard.
5. Show application review, tenant management, plan management, billing, versions, and support.
6. Open one tenant workspace.
7. Show the dashboard metrics.
8. Open members and create or view a borrower profile.
9. Open loan types, then create or view a loan.
10. Show automatic computation and amortization behavior.
11. Open loan payments and record a payment.
12. Show reports and available export formats.
13. If available, show overdue loan actions like reminder, penalty, restructure, or demand letter.
14. End in settings to show support requests, branding, and update tools.
